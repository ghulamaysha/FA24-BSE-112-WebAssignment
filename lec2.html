Lecture 2

Tiered Web Architecture

How an application is split into cooperating parts

Summary

This lecture explains how web applications are organised into tiers. It compares one-tier, two-tier and three-tier (N-tier) designs and shows how to choose between them by weighing scalability, maintainability and cost.

Topics Covered

  • Fundamentals
    • Tiers vs. layers
    • Separation of concerns
  • Architectures
    • One-tier (standalone) architecture with examples
    • Two-tier (client-server): advantages and limitations
    • Three-tier and N-tier architecture
  • Choosing an Architecture
    • Scalability
    • Maintainability
    • Cost trade-offs

Lecture 3

HTML and HTML5 Fundamentals

The markup that gives every page its structure

Summary

This lecture covers the role of markup and the structure of an HTML document. It introduces elements, tags and attributes, then moves through text, links, images, media, tables, lists and the new elements added in HTML5.

Topics Covered

  • Document Structure
    • Role of markup
    • DOCTYPE, head and body
    • Elements, tags, attributes, nesting and validation
  • Text and Links
    • Headings, paragraphs and text formatting
    • Absolute vs. relative hyperlinks
  • Content and HTML5
    • Images, media, tables and lists
    • HTML5 elements, attributes and browser support
